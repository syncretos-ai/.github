# Syncretos

Multi-model AI research with traceable evidence.

Most AI research tools hand you a confident paragraph and leave you to trust it. Syncretos
runs a question across a panel of models that draft, critique, and challenge each other,
then fuses the result into a synthesis where every claim points back to the passage it
came from — page and offset, not a plausible-looking citation.

Sources are parsed into structured claims **before** any model drafts, so the panel argues
over evidence rather than over recollection. When the sources do not support an answer, the
output says so instead of inventing one.

**Bring your own provider keys** — OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, or a custom
gateway — or use a managed plan. Your models, your costs, no single-vendor lock-in.

Currently in closed alpha, focused on the deliberation pipeline.
